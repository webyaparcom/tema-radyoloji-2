Questions

Questions before a scan

Information is general only; the decision on a scan and its preparation belong to your referring doctor and the radiologist. Always tell us if you might be pregnant.

0 / 6

01Do I need a referral?

Scans are done on a doctor’s request; bring your request form or e-prescription number.

02How early should I arrive?

15 minutes before; 30 minutes before for an MRI to fill in the safety form.

03Can I keep taking my medicines?

In most cases yes; if you take blood thinners or diabetes medicine, ask your doctor and tell us when booking.

04Could I be allergic to contrast?

If you have reacted before or have asthma, mention it when booking; the radiologist will assess it.

05What if I might be pregnant?

Always tell us before the scan; a scan with radiation is reconsidered with your doctor.

06When is my report ready?

For most scans 1–2 business days; the report is sent to your doctor and you get a text message (fictional timing).
